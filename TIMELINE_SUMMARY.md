## 🎓 Lessons from Timeline

### What Worked Well:
1. ✅ **Rapid MVP development** (Phases 1 & 1.5 in 3 days)
2. ✅ **Clear phase boundaries** and deliverables
3. ✅ **Realistic pivot** to India B2B market (Oct 2025)
4. ✅ **Documentation-first** approach

### Current Challenges:
1. ⚠️ **Phase 2 slower than Phase 1** (expected - real customers vs demo)
2. ⚠️ **Need customer validation** before major build
3. ⚠️ **Sales process not yet tested**

### Adjustments Made:
1. ✅ **Removed unrealistic features** (robotics, genomics, metaverse)
2. ✅ **B2B-first strategy** vs original B2C
3. ✅ **India-focused pricing** and GTM
4. ✅ **Extended timelines** to be realistic
